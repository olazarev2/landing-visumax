What is SMILE Pro

SMILE Pro is the latest advance in the flapless SMILE method. In a matter of seconds, the ZEISS VisuMax 800 femtosecond laser shapes a thin lens — a lenticule — inside the cornea, allowing it to be removed through a micro-incision of just 2 millimetres. The biomechanics of the cornea remain practically untouched.

SMILE Pro — a big step forward in vision correction

Put simply, it is the most modern and safe laser vision correction available today. It was created by the German company Carl Zeiss, known worldwide for its optics and medical devices. The SMILE method has been in use for more than ten years, and SMILE Pro is its improved version: more precise, gentler and faster.

The main difference is speed. The new VisuMax 800 laser works four times faster than previous models. Each eye takes only about 8 seconds. During this time the device carefully shapes an ultra-thin layer of tissue inside the cornea, which the doctor removes through a tiny 2 mm incision. Vision clears almost immediately, and as soon as the next day most patients return to their usual lives — behind the wheel, at work, on their smartphone screen.

SMILE Pro is not a replacement but a complement to the methods already used at "Microchirurgia ochiului": LASIK and other modern laser correction technologies. This allows the doctor to choose the optimal approach individually — based on the diagnostics and an in-person consultation.

How the procedure works

The entire correction takes 5–10 minutes per eye. The laser action itself lasts only 8 seconds.

Step 1

Preparing the patient

We clean the area around the eye, instil anaesthetic drops and place a soft eyelid speculum.

Step 2

Automatic centring

The AI systems CentraLign and OcuLign align the eye to the optical axis and account for the astigmatism axis.

Step 3

Shaping the lenticule

In 8 seconds the femtosecond laser shapes a thin lenticule disc and a 2 mm micro-incision to remove it.

Step 4

Removing the lenticule

The surgeon carefully extracts the lenticule through the micro-incision. The integrity of the cornea is preserved.

Step 5

Completion

We rinse the surgical area, remove the eyelid speculum, and provide a drop schedule and recommendations.

Indications and limitations

The method is suitable if you have:

  • Myopia from -1 to -10 dioptres
  • Astigmatism up to -5 dioptres
  • Age 18 and over
  • Stable vision over the past year
  • Sufficient corneal thickness

The procedure is not performed in case of:

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Progressive myopia
  • Inflammatory eye diseases
  • Autoimmune diseases
  • Keratoconus, cataract, glaucoma
  • Corneal thickness below 480 µm

Frequently asked questions

Does SMILE Pro correction hurt? +

The procedure is virtually painless thanks to drop anaesthesia. Most patients feel only a slight pressure during the 8 seconds of laser action.

How long does vision recovery take? +

Clarity returns within the first hours after surgery and improves significantly the next day, allowing you to return to work, driving and everyday tasks. Stabilisation occurs within 2–4 weeks.

Can both eyes be corrected in a single visit? +

Yes. SMILE Pro allows both eyes to be corrected on the same day — this is convenient and shortens the overall recovery period.

How does SMILE Pro differ from classic SMILE? +

SMILE Pro is performed on the new VisuMax 800 laser at a frequency of 2 MHz — four times faster than the previous generation, and it provides more precise centring thanks to AI assistants.

Is the method effective for astigmatism? +

Yes, SMILE Pro corrects astigmatism up to -5 dioptres. The OcuLign system automatically accounts for the torsional shift of the axis.

When can I return to working at the computer? +

As early as the next day. We recommend taking a break every hour and observing eye hygiene during the first weeks.
